# Introduction

This project tries to bridge moabb datasets along with our self-collected data and a torch training loop
in a most modular and customizable fashion to allow:
- Training / Testing on various (possible multiple at once) moabb datasets (along with our own data).
- Playing around with various EEG preprocessing (windowing, STFT, VMD, etc.) and augmentation techniques.
- Utmost simple, yet, in, to a right balance, utmost customizable prototyping with 
various DL architectures and various training approaches.
